# What you need

A cinefit system is a PC in your rack, between your sources and your projector. Here is each part, what it has to do, and the options to buy where there is a choice.

<ol class="parts-toc"><li><a href="#pc"><span class="name">A PC</span><span class="pick">a desktop with two free PCIe slots; tested with an AMD Ryzen 5 5600X and 16 GB of memory</span></a></li><li><a href="#gpu"><span class="name">An AMD graphics card</span><span class="pick">tested with the Radeon RX 6400; newer AMD cards should work</span></a></li><li><a href="#capture"><span class="name">A Blackmagic DeckLink capture card</span><span class="pick">tested with the Mini Recorder 4K; the Quad HDMI Recorder should work</span></a></li><li><a href="#source"><span class="name">A source the capture card accepts</span><span class="pick">a signal without HDCP, through an HDMI splitter</span></a></li><li><a href="#display"><span class="name">A projector or TV</span><span class="pick">a display with HDMI in</span></a></li><li><a href="#remote"><span class="name">A remote</span><span class="pick">optional; tested with a flirc USB receiver</span></a></li></ol>

## 1. A PC {#pc}

<p>Runs Ubuntu and cinefit, with no desktop: cinefit starts by itself when the PC powers on, and after setup it needs no monitor or keyboard of its own.</p><ul class="need-facts"><li><span class="k">Needs</span><span>Two PCIe slots, one for each card below; Ubuntu 26.04</span></li><li><span class="k">CPU</span><span>From about 2015 on (Intel Haswell, AMD Excavator or Zen) for the published builds; older ones can <a href="/build-from-source/">build from source</a></span></li><li><span class="k">Tested</span><span>AMD Ryzen 5 5600X, 16 GB of memory</span></li><li><span class="k">Check</span><span>Room and airflow for two cards</span></li></ul>

## 2. An AMD graphics card {#gpu}

<p>Scales, crops and colors every frame, and drives the projector. Developed and tested on an <strong>AMD Radeon RX 6400</strong>, chosen on purpose as a modest card: if cinefit runs well on it, most AMD cards will do.</p><ul class="need-facts"><li><span class="k">Needs</span><span>An AMD card with Vulkan and HDMI out</span></li><li><span class="k">Tested</span><span>RX 6400 only</span></li><li><span class="k">Not tried</span><span>NVIDIA, Intel</span></li></ul>

## The finer print

For anyone matching parts or troubleshooting:

- **GPU**: needs Vulkan (Mesa's RADV driver on AMD), a display output the Linux kernel drives directly, and an overlay plane for the on-screen menu. cinefit holds the GPU's clocks steady on AMD only; elsewhere it logs `gpu clocks: not held` and carries on.
- **Capture driver**: Blackmagic Desktop Video 16.0 or newer.
- **CPU**: published builds target x86-64-v3 (AVX2).
- **Nothing else on the display**: no desktop environment on the GPU's output. cinefit drives the display itself.
- **Optional extras**: a Denon receiver and an Apple TV on your network add input names and what's playing to the on-screen info. See [Receiver and Apple TV](/integrations/).
